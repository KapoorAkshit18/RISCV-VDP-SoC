### Audit Conclusions

1. **Independent Verification Complete**: All 10 mutant behaviors are independently verified without relying on internal sequence classifications. 
2. **Methodology Enforced**: The sequences operate as true "blind" oracles. They expect golden functionality according to the specification and report UVM errors solely based on deviations from that standard.
3. **Equivalence Accurately Identified**: M05 and M09 are mathematically confirmed as functionally Equivalent Mutants because their defects reside in signal paths that are explicitly masked or ignored by the receiving Native-Bus Slave IP blocks.
4. **No Escapes/Unobservables in Active UVM**: Due to the comprehensive mapping of the Active UVM sequences, all 8 structurally viable mutations were activated, observed, and caught. No viable mutations escaped.
